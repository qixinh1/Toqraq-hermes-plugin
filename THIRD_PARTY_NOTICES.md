# Third-Party Notices

The Toqraq base plugin does not bundle third-party runtime code. FastAPI,
Pydantic, Starlette, WebSockets, React, and the Hermes SDK are supplied by the
Hermes host. Development tools and their transitive packages are not included
in the installed plugin.

Managed engines and tools are separate, digest-identified components. Each
admitted component must carry its own license, copyright, attribution, source,
and build-provenance notices inside that component package.
